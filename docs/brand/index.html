The Peal family's marks

Four marks on one 24-unit grid with a 2-unit stroke. Each holds one solid dot, the thing that product keeps hold of: the bowl of the P, the seal on a payment order, the wax seal on an envelope, the agent on its leash. In full colour only the dot, and Bidder's limit line, carries colour.

At size

The favicon tile at 16 and 32 pixels, the mark at 24, 32, 64 and 128.

Lockups on light, dark and in one colour

Rules

  • Use the files as they are. Do not redraw, outline, rotate, add effects or recolour beyond the light, dark and mono versions.
  • Clear space around a mark is half its height. A bare mark is at least 18 px; smaller than that, use the tile.
  • "Peal" is IBM Plex Sans SemiBold, the product name Regular, as outlines. Product pages may set their own display type; the lockup stays as drawn.
  • The mono files use currentColor, so they take the colour of the text around them.
