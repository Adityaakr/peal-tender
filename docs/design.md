# The Peal family: shared rules

Revised 16 November 2026 (third revision). The three hackathon entries (Peal Tender,
Peal Sealed Auction, Peal Bidder) are **one family with three different faces**. Each
product designs its own identity from its own subject: palette, typefaces, layout,
illustration, hero concept and motion are its own, and no product reuses another's page
structure. This file holds only what they share. Earlier revisions that prescribed one
look for all three are withdrawn.

## How each product starts its design

Write a short concept before drawing anything, and put it at the top of the pull
request that introduces the design:

1. **The visitor:** who arrives and what they already know.
2. **The idea in five seconds:** one sentence the page must land.
3. **The metaphor:** taken from the product's own world (its documents, instruments,
   rituals, units), not from other websites.

Then pick the palette, the type pairing and the layout from that metaphor, check the
page in a real browser at desktop and phone widths, critique it against **What is
out**, and revise at least twice before opening the pull request.

## What is shared

- **The family name and wordmark.** Each product is "Peal <thing>". In each product's
  footer, a family line names all three, each with its own mark and a link:
  - Peal Tender: the treasury (github.com/Adityaakr/peal-tender)
  - Peal Sealed Auction: the venue (github.com/Adityaakr/peal-sealed-auction)
  - Peal Bidder: the participant (github.com/Adityaakr/peal-bidder)

  The current product is marked as the current one. Credit line: "Design language
  started from Peal."
- **Logos.** The marks, wordmarks, lockups and favicons are in
  [`docs/brand/`](brand/), with a brand sheet at `docs/brand/index.html`. Use the files
  as drawn; never redraw a mark in page code.
  - **One system.**
    - Every mark is drawn on a 24-unit grid with a 2-unit stroke and round joins.
    - Every mark holds one solid dot, the thing that product keeps hold of:
      - Peal: the bowl of the P;
      - Tender: the seal on a payment order;
      - Sealed Auction: the wax seal on an envelope;
      - Bidder: the agent on its leash, short of the red limit line.
    - In full colour only the dot, and Bidder's limit line, carries the product's colour.
  - **Versions.** For each of Peal, Tender, Sealed Auction and Bidder:
    - `mark`, `wordmark` and `lockup`, each in light, `-dark` and `-mono` (`currentColor`);
    - `tile.svg` and `favicon.svg`;
    - favicon PNGs at 32, 180 and 512 px.
  - **Wordmark.** "Peal" in IBM Plex Sans SemiBold, then the product name in Regular,
    both as outlines. A product page may set its own display type around the lockup,
    not inside it.
  - **Size.** Clear space is half the mark's height. A bare mark is at least 18 px;
    smaller than that (favicons), use the tile.
  - **Code.** The web apps take the marks from a module generated by
    `docs/brand/build.mjs`, so pages and files never drift apart.
- **Voice.**
  - Sentence case, present tense, short sentences.
  - Specific: real names (`pqcVerify`, ML-DSA-87, ML-KEM-1024), real units, real
    numbers with their source.
  - Tell people the command when an action needs one.
- **Evidence.** A figure is read from the chain, taken from a named transaction or test
  run, or labelled as a target. Hashes and addresses are shown in full on hover and
  copy on click.
- **Accessibility and spacing baseline.**
  - Works at 390, 768, 1280 and 1440 pixels wide; the page itself never scrolls
    sideways (only a table, a diagram or code inside its own scroller).
  - Side gutters at least 16px.
  - Text contrast at least WCAG AA.
  - Every control keyboard-reachable with a visible focus ring.
  - Status never by colour alone (an icon or a word too).
  - Diagrams are `role="img"` with an `aria-label`.
  - Everything readable is visible at rest; `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` gets a
    complete still page.
- **Trust.** Treasury and committee keys never enter a web page. Text from the chain or
  from files people send is set with `textContent`, never as HTML.
- **Wallets.**
  - One Connect control opens the product's wallet surface, styled in that product's
    identity.
  - It offers what QoreChain actually supports and says what each can do:
    - a **guest account**: a testnet key pair made in the browser, with backup and
      forget; it registers its ML-DSA-87 key and opens scoped windows;
    - **QoreX**: identity, and funding the guest with one transfer;
    - **MetaMask**: identity and the network; it cannot sign for QoreChain by itself.
  - It shows each step it takes and errors in words.

## What is out (for every product)

- Gradient hero backgrounds, glows, blobs, meshes; glassmorphism; neon; gradient text.
- Rows of identical cards; grids of icons in tinted squares; emoji; everything centred.
- The common generated looks: warm cream with a serif and a terracotta accent; near-black
  with one acid colour; broadsheet hairline rules with dense columns; purple-to-blue on
  white; an accent bar on rounded cards; the same radius and shadow on every block.
- Numbered markers that do not encode a real sequence.
- Marketing words without a measurement ("unlock", "revolutionise", "seamless",
  "next-gen", "cutting-edge", "powerful", "robust"); em-dashes in interface copy;
  exclamation marks.
- Invented numbers; motion that loops for no reason, bounces or shakes.
