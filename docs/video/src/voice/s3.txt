Here's how a buyback works. The treasury deposits its payment token, and opens a tender: a budget, and a ladder of prices. Holders offer their tokens at the lowest price they'll accept. The price climbs while the budget can still pay every offer at or below it. At settlement, everyone who sells is paid that one clearing price, never less than they asked. The vault's first tender cleared at zero point zero zero one three, and bought back seventy-six point nine two tokens.
