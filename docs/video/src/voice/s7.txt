Version two moves authority to three signers. Any two of their M L D S A keys sign each order. Payments draw on a daily cap. Anything larger, and every rule change, waits in a public timelock, where one honest signer can veto it. Anyone can settle a finished tender. And a buyback can run as a sealed sale on Peal Sealed Auction, with bids hidden until the close, and proceeds that can only return to the vault.
