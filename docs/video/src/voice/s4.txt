So why can't a stolen key touch it? Every action that moves money, or changes the rules, carries a fresh M L D S A signature, bound to the chain, the vault, the action, a number that works only once, and the exact arguments. The vault reads the signer's key with P Q C key status, and checks the signature with P Q C verify: Core Chain's own precompiles. And every transaction goes out through an E V M window scoped to the vault, then closed.
